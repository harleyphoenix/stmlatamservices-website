About us

STM LATAM Services LLC is a single-member consulting company owned by Harley Dirk Bieder, an entrepreneur with over 30 years of international business experience and long-standing hands-on involvement in North, Central and South America. Many ventures and relocations fail not because the opportunity was wrong, but because the context was misunderstood – we help clients approach Latin America with clarity, realistic expectations and risk awareness.

Consultations are held online via video call and in person. Every engagement is based on independent, practical experience gathered on the ground in the countries we cover.

Why clients work with us

  • One personal point of contact from first call to final step
  • First-hand knowledge from regular trips across the region
  • Fees agreed in advance, per consultation or package
  • Clear, honest information – including the downsides of each country

Services

We advise two groups of clients: entrepreneurs doing business in the region, and individuals planning to live there.

For entrepreneurs & business owners

Market orientation

Understanding local market dynamics, customer behaviour, competition and regional differences before committing time and capital.

Business culture advisory

How business is actually done in Latin America: communication styles, negotiation norms, timelines and expectations of local partners.

Partner & opportunity assessment

Independent, experience-based insight to evaluate potential partners, suppliers and commercial opportunities for credibility and feasibility.

On-the-ground briefings

First-hand insights from within the region on local developments and real-world conditions that affect business planning.

Strategic sounding board

A confidential advisory relationship for founders making decisions about expansion, operations or long-term positioning in the region.

For individuals & families

Country selection

Comparison of destination countries by cost of living, safety, climate, healthcare, infrastructure and quality of life – matched to each client's personal situation.

Residency options

General information on the residency categories available in each country (e.g. retiree, passive income, investor and digital nomad programs) and their typical requirements.

Relocation planning

A practical roadmap: documents to prepare, timeline, housing, schools, healthcare and insurance, and what to expect during the first months.

Living & housing

Orientation on neighbourhoods, rental and property markets and everyday life, drawing on long-standing experience in real estate and construction.

Plan B strategy

Advice for people who want a second base abroad as a safeguard – how to structure a step-by-step plan that fits their life.

Partner referrals

Where clients need legal, immigration, tax or banking services, they are referred to independent licensed professionals and contract with them directly.

Scope of our services

STM LATAM Services LLC does not

  • open bank accounts on behalf of clients
  • form companies in any jurisdiction
  • provide nominee directors, shareholders or addresses
  • hold, receive or transfer client funds
  • act as an intermediary in immigration or banking procedures
